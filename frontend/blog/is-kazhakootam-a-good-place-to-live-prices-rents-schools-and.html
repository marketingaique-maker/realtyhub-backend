Is Kazhakootam a Good Place to Live? Prices, Rents, Schools and Connectivity Explained

Admin User · 25 Oct 2026

Kazhakootam is one of the most talked-about localities in Trivandrum, and the reason is easy to see. Technopark is located here, the National Highway passes through it, and a mix of educational institutions, residential streets, and everyday services has grown around it over the years.

For anyone considering a move, whether to rent, to buy a plot, or to settle permanently, a few practical questions usually come up first. What do plots and rentals cost? Where are the schools? How is the area connected to the rest of the city? This guide covers these points in a simple, balanced way, so that an informed decision can be made.

Why Kazhakootam Is Considered a Popular Place to Live

Kazhakootam is generally regarded as a convenient location for several reasons:

Proximity to Technopark: Employment is concentrated in and around the IT park, so a short commute is possible for many residents.

Road connectivity: NH66 passes through the area, and several junctions provide access to other parts of the city.

Established surroundings: Educational institutions, shops, eateries, and services have developed nearby.

A mix of housing options: Plots, independent houses, apartments, and rental homes are all found in and around the area.

It should be noted that no locality suits everyone. Traffic around the IT corridor can be heavy at peak hours, and conditions differ from one street to another. Visits at different times of day are recommended before a decision is made.

Land and Plot Prices in Kazhakootam

Land in Kerala is typically sold by the cent (1 cent is approximately 435.6 square feet), and asking rates are influenced by several factors:

  • Distance from Technopark and the main junctions
  • Road frontage and width of the access road
  • Plot shape, size, and levelling
  • Surrounding development and the condition of the neighbourhood

In recent Realty Hub listings in Kazhakootam, Karyavattom, and Pullanivila, asking rates have generally been seen in the range of about ₹7 lakh to ₹18 lakh per cent, with plots closer to main roads and junctions usually being priced higher. These are asking rates, not guaranteed sale prices, and they change over time. Current rates should always be confirmed directly for the specific plot being considered.

For anyone considering a purchase, a guide to the documents required to buy land in Kerala is recommended reading before any advance is paid. Registration and stamp duty information is published by the Kerala Registration Department, and current requirements should be verified there.

Houses and Independent Homes

Independent houses, both older and newly built, are also offered in the area. Prices for houses are affected by plot size, building age, construction quality, and location, so a wide range is seen. A structural check by a qualified civil engineer is commonly advised before a resale house is purchased. The differences between buying land and buying a built home are explained in the guide on plot vs ready-built house in Kerala.

Rents in Kazhakootam

Rental homes are in constant demand around Technopark, mainly because of the large working population. Furnished, semi-furnished, and unfurnished options are available, including apartments and independent houses.

Rents vary with the size of the home, furnishing, building facilities, and the distance from the IT park. In Realty Hub's recent rental listings, homes near Technopark have been advertised with starting points of around ₹12,000 per month. Actual rents depend on the specific property, and the asking rent is not always the final agreed rent.

Apart from monthly rent, other costs are usually accounted for by renters, such as:

  • Security deposit
  • Maintenance charges
  • Electricity and water bills
  • Internet
  • Parking
  • Brokerage, if applicable

More detail is provided in the guide to renting near Technopark, and available homes can be browsed through Realty Hub's property listings.

Schools and Education Near Kazhakootam

Education is one of the main reasons families consider this part of Trivandrum. Several schools and colleges are located in and around the area, and the University of Kerala's Karyavattom campus is a well-known institution nearby. The University of Kerala provides official information about its programmes and campuses.

Because schools differ in curriculum (State, CBSE, or ICSE), medium of instruction, admission procedures, and fees, the following points should be checked directly with each school:

  • Curriculum and medium of instruction
  • Admission process and eligibility
  • Distance from the home being considered
  • School transport availability
  • Fees and other charges

A list of schools is not given here, since details change and should be verified from official school sources.

Healthcare and Daily Needs

Pharmacies, clinics, grocery stores, and eateries are commonly found along the main roads and junctions in and around Kazhakootam. For specialised care, hospitals in other parts of Trivandrum are also used by residents. Before a home is chosen, it is advisable that the nearest hospital, pharmacy, and market be located and the route to each checked.

Connectivity and Transport

Connectivity is one of the strongest points of the area, though it should be noted that conditions can change as roads are developed and upgraded.

National Highway: NH66 passes through Kazhakootam, providing north-south connectivity.

Key junctions: Kazhakootam Junction, Karyavattom Junction, and Pullanivila are commonly used reference points. Access to MC Road (Narikkal) is also available from nearby areas.

Public transport: Bus services operate in and around the area. Routes and timings are published by KSRTC and should be checked for the specific route needed.

Railway and air travel: Kochuveli railway station and Trivandrum International Airport are commonly used by residents of the area. Distances and travel times should be checked for the specific location.

Technopark: The IT park is reached directly from the area, and employment information is published by Technopark.

The real commute should be tested at the hours it will actually be used, since peak-hour traffic can be very different from what a map estimate suggests.

Who May Find Kazhakootam a Good Fit

The area is often considered by:

IT professionals who wish to stay close to work

Young couples and small families looking for rental homes or a first plot

Families who value access to schools, shops, and services

NRIs and investors looking at land in a growing locality, though returns are never guaranteed

Things to Consider Before Moving or Buying

Traffic and noise: Busy junctions and main roads may be noisier than interior streets.

Drainage and waterlogging: Visiting after rain is a simple way to check for drainage or leakage problems.

Documents: Title, tax receipts, and survey details should be verified for any property, ideally with a lawyer.

Budget: Not just the purchase price or rent, but all related costs, should be counted.

Future plans: Whether the stay is short-term or long-term should be decided, since it affects whether renting or buying is the better choice.
