Why Kazhakkootam Is One of the Best Places to Invest in Real Estate in Trivandrum in 2026

Admin User · 15/09/2026

If you've been watching Trivandrum's property market over the last few years, one locality keeps coming up in conversation: Kazhakkootam. What used to be a quiet stretch along NH66 has turned into one of the city's most active real estate corridors driven almost entirely by its position next to Technopark, Kerala's largest IT employment hub.

In this guide, we'll break down exactly what's driving real estate investment in Kazhakkootam, what price trends look like heading into 2026, and how to think about buying here whether you're a first-time buyer, an NRI, or a long-term investor.

Infrastructure & Connectivity

Kazhakkootam sits directly on NH66, giving it strong north-south connectivity across the city and beyond. Within the locality itself, a few junctions matter a lot for property value:

Karyavattom Junction : a key connector point with growing commercial activity

Kazhakkootam Junction : the main hub, close to Technopark's side gate

Pullanivila and MC Road (Narikkal) : slightly quieter, more residential stretches, still within easy reach of the IT corridor

Sreekaryam–Pothencode Road : an alternate route gaining relevance as the area develops

Plots directly facing NH66 or within a kilometre or two of these junctions tend to hold and appreciate value faster, since accessibility is often the first thing both homebuyers and renters check.

Price Trends & ROI

Potential

Land in Kazhakkootam is typically sold by the cent (1 cent ≈ 435 sq. ft.), and pricing varies a fair amount depending on proximity to Technopark and the main junctions:

  • Plots close to Technopark's gate or Karyavattom Junction: roughly ₹10–18 lakh per cent
  • Slightly interior plots near Pullanivila, MC Road, or Sreekaryam,Pothencode Road: roughly ₹7–12 lakh per cent
  • Larger parcels (10+ cents) further from the main junctions: can start lower, around ₹6–8 lakh per cent

These are indicative ranges based on current listing patterns, actual rates shift plot by plot depending on road frontage, shape, and exact distance to landmarks, so it's worth checking current plots for sale in Kazhakkootam for live pricing rather than relying on averages alone.

What makes the ROI case stronger here than in many other Trivandrum localities is simple: as Technopark's campuses continue to expand, the pool of people who need to live nearby keeps growing too, which supports both resale value and rental yield.

Who Should Invest Here

Kazhakkootam isn't a one-size-fits-all locality, different buyers are drawn here for different reasons:

  • IT professionals (25–35) buying their first home, often prioritizing a short commute to Technopark over plot size
  • NRIs looking for a stable, appreciating asset in Kerala with strong rental demand from the IT workforce even while they're abroad
  • Mid-career professionals and families (35–50) upgrading from a rented flat to owned land or a house, often looking slightly further from the main junctions for more space and quieter surroundings
  • Long-term investors buying larger plots now with the expectation of subdividing or developing later as the corridor continues to grow

If you fall into the NRI category, it's worth reviewing Kerala's property registration and stamp duty process in advance, since documentation requirements can differ slightly for overseas buyers.

Nearby Landmarks That Add Value

Beyond Technopark itself, a few landmarks consistently show up as value-drivers in this area. Greenfield International Stadium has brought both visibility and infrastructure improvements to the surrounding roads. The University of Kerala's Karyavattom campus adds a steady student and academic population nearby. And the concentration of IT employers, Infosys, UST, and the broader Kinfra ecosystem, means the area isn't reliant on Technopark alone for its economic base.

Together, these give Kazhakkootam a mix of residential, educational, and commercial anchors that most single-purpose localities in Trivandrum don't have.

How to Choose the Right Plot in Kazhakkootam

A few practical things worth checking before you commit to any plot here:

  • Road access and frontage : a plot with direct, all-weather road access will always be easier to build on and resell than one accessible only through a narrow lane
  • Distance to your actual daily landmarks : "near Technopark" can mean anywhere from 1 km to 5 km, so ask for the exact distance, not just the general claim
  • Documentation : confirm clear title, verify survey numbers, and check for any pending encumbrances before paying any advance
  • Future connectivity plans : roads and junctions that are currently under improvement (like severa
l stretches near MC Road) can meaningfully affect value over the next few years

If you're also considering renting rather than buying immediately, it's worth comparing against rental properties near Technopark to see what monthly costs look like versus the long-term cost of ownership.

Frequently Asked Questions
